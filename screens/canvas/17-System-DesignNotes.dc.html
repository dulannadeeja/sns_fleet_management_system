Plate · how SNS Rent Car looks

Plate is the current and only visual system, chosen by the owner on 24 Oct 2026. It sets the colours, type, navigation frame, icons and vehicle images; screens, steps, words and money rules come from the system design.

One test for every choice: can Nimal read it on his phone at the yard at noon, and can nothing be mistaken for a vehicle status?

භූමිය · Cool grey ground

Paper #eef1f0 under white cards. The warm cream was dropped: it read as a default look, and the grey lets the yellow plate carry the warmth.

අංකය · The plate is the signature

Yellow plate face #f6c343 with ink, only on number plates, the SNS mark and the active nav pill. Staff find a car by its plate before reading a word.

ක්‍රියා · Action blue, never a status

Action blue #1756a9 for buttons, links and focus only, 7.1:1 with white text. Status colours outrank it and always carry an icon and a word.

සංචලනය · Navigation

Computer: a 264px labelled sidebar (chosen 25 Oct 2026, after Jobber and Shopify). The logo, then the items grouped by job: today, cars and rentals, money, running SNS, with Settings at the bottom. Items are still trimmed by permission. Counts sit at the end of a line: red only for overdue, dark for things waiting. The top bar holds the page title, search, a “New rental” button for anyone who can rent, the සිං / EN switch and the account (initials only below 1,100px). The sidebar is always on the left and stays in view while the page scrolls; below 1,100px it becomes a 104px rail, icon over the Sinhala label. Phone screens use PageHeader (back link) inside flows and BottomTabBar at the top level.

Phone: four labelled tabs. Staff also get one raised centre action, "අලුත්" (new rental), because starting a rental is their most frequent task. The owner's phone has no centre action.

The screens behind each item, and the order of steps inside them, follow the system design.
