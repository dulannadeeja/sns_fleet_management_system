# app/src/modules/: business rules and database access (no React)

One folder per PRD module. Each change function checks the person's permission again inside its own transaction and writes the activity log there (TRD › Integrity rules). Rules in `app/AGENTS.md` apply here too.

- The activity log (`src/modules/activity/`, screen 15.5) is read only: no code changes or deletes an entry. Its page needs `seeActivityLog` (the admin by default); kinds come from the action's name (`person.*` is Settings, and so on); every action written to `audit_event` needs a sentence under `settings.log.actions` in both word files (`tests/log-words.test.ts`).
- Owner settings (`src/modules/settings/`, screens 15.2 and 15.7): the catalogue holds each setting's group, limits and starting value; `setting` keeps only values the owner changed. Only the owner changes them (`changeGroup` checks again in its transaction and logs `setting.change` with old and new); owner, admin and staff read them (`requireEmployee`). Settings are shared by the whole test database: a test changes only its own setting and puts it back; the exceptions are the business details (a fourth group, #55), the handover accessories (a fifth, #57, kind `lines`: one item a line, `linesOf`) and the fuel price (#63): when none are set, `tests/helpers/business.ts` and `tests/helpers/handover.ts` set sample ones and leave them, as printing, handover and same-level returns need them.
- Cars (`src/modules/cars/`, screens 04.1–04.4, 04.8): a car's package and terms are dated versions in `vehicle_terms_version` (insert-only); a change adds one, so rentals keep theirs (AT-05). The commission is a partner term: seen with `seePartnerEarnings`, changed with `editPartners`; history and the log hide it from others. Pages use `AppFrame` and `railItems` (`src/app/rail.ts`). Starting packages (`packages.ts`, screen 04.6 at `/settings/packages`): a name, rates and models (a model can be in several; a new car picks one, `vehicle.starting_package_id`), and one `package_default` row; every employee reads them, `editCarsAndPackages` changes them (the commission default also `editPartners`); a model can be taken out (a package keeps one); logged `package.*` with old and new; packages are never removed. Tests add sample packages (names starting "Sample") for sample models only, never the real ones; nobody may add or edit packages under another prefix in tests.
- Car status (`cars/status.ts`, screen 04.5 at `/cars/[id]/status`): insert-only `vehicle_status_event`, the newest is the status (none is Available); `markCarUnavailable` sets Under repair or Unavailable, `markCarAvailable` marks available, the admin or owner role retires, only the owner brings a retired car back; `statusChoices` (`src/app/cars/status-choices.ts`) offers only what the person may do, and `changeStatus` checks again under the per-car lock. Logged `car.status`. Rented and Overdue come from rentals (Phase 2).
- Papers and photo (`cars/papers.ts`): insurance and licence expiry, next service date and km are four `vehicle` columns (the app may update only these and the partner link); every employee changes them at `/cars/[id]/papers`, logged `car.papers`. The catalogue photo is insert-only `vehicle_photo` (newest row wins; no file = removed), needs `editCarsAndPackages`, JPEG/PNG/WebP by its bytes up to 10 MB, private storage, shown only through `/cars/[id]/photo/[photoId]` to employees (not logged: not a customer document). `CarPicture` shows it or `VehicleImage`. A plain `<img>`: next/image would fetch it without the sign-in.
- Partners (`src/modules/partners/`, screens 11.1–11.3): a partner is a `user` (role partner) plus `partner_owner`; adding one links partner cars not yet linked (`linkCarToPartner` in the cars module, under the per-car lock) in one transaction. The commission and the costs the owner pays are partner terms. Pages need `seePartnerEarnings`; changes `editPartners`. The app role may set only a car's owner link and a partner's agreement date and bank account.
- Go-live checklist (`src/modules/golive/`, screen 01.3 at `/settings/golive`, owner only): ten items; `readChecklist` checks car dates (fleet = cars not retired), partner links and the authorised person by itself; the owner ticks the rest (`golive_tick`, insert-only, newest per item; unticking needs a reason; logged `golive.tick`/`untick`); items not built yet (`kind: 'later'`) never count as done. Going live itself is Phase 4. Ticks are shared by the test database: tests flip their own item and put it back.
- Customers (`src/modules/customers/`, screens 08.1–08.3): one NIC is one customer (AT-51); copies are private files (`src/storage/`: R2, or a private folder outside production), checked from their own bytes (`storage/kinds.ts`), insert-only rows; opening one goes only through `/customers/[id]/copies/[docId]`, which records it in `document_access` (AT-59). Everything needs `customerAccess`. Flags (`customers/flags.ts`, 08.5): `customer_flag` is insert-only except closing it once (a trigger), logged `customer.flagAdd`/`flagClose`; `warningsFor` is what New rental will read (Phase 2). The check before a rental (08.4, `/customers/[id]/check`) records `customer_go_ahead` (insert-only) only if the warnings shown still hold; a flag needs `proceedDespiteFlag`, an expired licence `overrideExpiredDocuments`.
- Rentals (`src/modules/rentals/`, #50 foundation): `createRental` saves a draft with its own terms: the car's newest terms copied onto `rental_vehicle_segment` (never changed afterwards: a trigger allows only the km readings) and the owner's cutoff, deadline and fuel price on `rental_terms_snapshot` (insert-only). Every calculation reads these (`rentalTerms`, `rentalEstimate`), never the car's live package. The car is held in `vehicle_hold` from pickup to deadline; the exclusion constraint refuses overlaps (`carTaken`), also for two saves at once. Deadlines and estimates are pure functions in `src/lib/rental-days.ts` (Asia/Colombo, fixed UTC+05:30). Saving needs Customer access; the pickup is the moment of the save (8 Nov 2026; a deadline that moved since it was shown is refused, `shownDeadline`); 1 to 90 days; `draft` until handover, then `active` (#57); return and cancel add theirs (system design 7.1, Confirmed 7 Nov 2026). The commission on a segment is a partner term: callers filter it. Logged `rental.*`. Tests use a pickup a few minutes ahead and their own sample car (`tests/helpers/rentals.ts`). New rental (#51, `/rentals/new`, boards 05.1 to 05.3): the customer step reads `warningsFor`; with warnings the decision is made there (`GoAheadForm forRental`) and `createRental` takes its id (`goAheadId`): it must cover exactly the warnings now and is used by one rental (`rental.go_ahead_id` unique); a first rental (no rental of theirs ever went out; drafts and cancelled ones do not count) needs `approveFirstCustomer`. `freeCarsNow` offers Available cars nothing holds during a one-day rental from now; the pickup is the moment of the save. `/rentals/[id]` shows a saved rental until 06.2 (#58). Step 4 (#54, `/rentals/[id]/terms`, board 05.4; `rentals/terms.ts`): `setRentalTerms` saves the deposit (LKR 1 to 500,000) or its waiver with a reason (a new or changed one needs `setOrWaiveDeposit`), rent paid now or at return, fuel (same level needs the fuel price saved with the rental), reference needed and which (these need `customerAccess` only); changeable while draft; logged `rental.terms` and a `rental_event`. `rentalReadiness` says what is missing (the handover check, #57, reads it). Reference people (`customers/references.ts`, board 05.6 at `/rentals/[id]/reference`): kept with the customer, one NIC per customer, copies (NIC front and back, licence) insert-only in `reference_document`, opened only through `/customers/[id]/references/[refId]/copies/[docId]` (logged `customer.referenceCopyOpened`). The agreement (#55, `rentals/agreements.ts`; 05.5 at `/rentals/[id]/review`, printouts 16.1/16.2 at `/rentals/[id]/agreement/[printId]`, Settings › Agreement wording at `/settings/agreement`): only the owner adds and approves a wording version (`agreement_wording`; approving once is the only change a trigger allows); the newest approved one is printed; printing needs step 4, an approved version and the business details, and keeps what it printed in `agreement_print.content` (insert-only), in the chosen language, with the rental's number (`rental.number`, shown R-0001); the signed copy is `agreement_signed` (insert-only, private, opened through `/rentals/[id]/signed/[signedId]`, logged) and names the printout it signs (`print_id`): only the newest printout, and only if printed after the last change to printed terms (`reprintFirst`); `rental.terms_set_at` moves only when the deposit, rent timing or fuel change; `newestPrint` reads both; `agreementState` says printed, signed, or out of date (a newer printout or changed terms). Approving a version older than the current one is refused (`olderThanCurrent`). The go-live items business details and agreement wording are now checked by the system. Logged `document.*` and `rental.agreement*`. Tests leave approved sample wording in place.
- Money (`src/modules/money/`, #53): `recordPayment` (09.3, 09.4 at `/rentals/[id]/pay`; Customer access) saves one `payment` per part (cash, bank with its reference, card with its receipt number), for a bill (`payment_allocation`, shared across the customer's rentals, each at most what is unpaid on its estimate until return) or a deposit (`deposit_movement` kind `received`, at most the deposit set less what was received; held money, never income). A cash part adds a `cash_movement` (`paymentIn`); a bank or card part a `reconciliation` row `waiting`. All five tables are insert-only. The form's key (`idempotency_key` = key and part) records a form sent twice once; locks: `sns.customer:<id>`, then each `sns.rental:<id>` in order (`setRentalTerms` locks the rental too and refuses a deposit below what was received, `depositBelowReceived`). A reference already on another payment of the same method returns `sameReference` with those payments; saving needs a reason (`same_reference_reason`). Numbers are P-0001 (`paymentNumber`). `PAYABLE_STATES` lists the rental states a payment can go on (draft and active; return adds its own with #63). Logged `payment.record` (hidden, with `rental.*`, from people without Customer access) and a `rental_event` `payment`. `rentalMoney` and `payableRentals` read it. The bill cap reads the estimate now: a later ticket that changes days or cars on a rental (#61, #62) must keep the paid amount within the bill or say what happens to the rest.
- Handover (`rentals/handover.ts`, #57; 06.4 at `/rentals/[id]/handover`, 06.5 on the rental page): `handoverChecklist` lists what blocks (`missing`: the customer's four copies, a signed and current agreement, the reference person and their copies, the car Available, an accessories list in Settings, the car not out on another rental (`carOut`), the return time not passed (`deadlinePassed`)) and shows money (never blocking). `handOver` (Customer access) locks the car then the rental, checks it all again, needs `checked` (staff confirm they checked the car; items start unticked), refuses an odometer below the car's last reading (`lastKmOf`: the most recent handover or return reading; `kmLower`) or a list changed since shown (`listChanged`); past or missing insurance or licence dates need `overrideExpiredDocuments` and a reason. It saves `rental_handover` (insert-only, one per rental: km, fuel of five steps, the list as ticked), the segment's `out_km`, and the rental `active`; logged `rental.handover` and a `rental_event`. A car out stays with its rental until it is back, also overdue (Derived control): `carIsOut` (rentals.ts) refuses a new rental (`carTaken`) and `freeCarsNow` leaves it out; cars show Rented (`Car.rented`). Not collected (`notCollected`, 2 hours after saving, still draft): with nothing paid, `freeCar` (reason, car then rental locks) ends the hold and sets `notCollected` (logged `rental.carFreed`); something paid waits for cancel (#59). Condition photos: `handover_photo` (insert-only, private, up to 10, only before handover), listed on the rental page afterwards, opened through `/rentals/[id]/photos/[photoId]` and logged (`rental.photoOpened`). Readers pass `q` (the transaction) to `getCustomer`, `rentalReadiness` and `agreementState`, so the check runs on one connection. `tests/helpers/handover.ts` · `readyRental` makes a rental ready to hand over.
- Rentals list and details (`rentals/list.ts`, #58; 06.1 at `/rentals`, 06.2 and 06.3 on `/rentals/[id]`): `listRentals` groups open rentals (draft and active) into overdue (out past the deadline; `minutesLate` rounds up), due back by the next return deadline (`nextDeadlineAfter`, `src/lib/rental-days.ts`), handover in progress (drafts, with their checklist progress and "Not collected") and the rest; Returned today comes with #63. The only filter is `overdue` (AT-28): the menu's Rentals item shows the count (`overdueCount`, only for people with Customer access) and links to `/rentals?filter=overdue`; the filter shows and can be removed. The details page shows the overdue banner (a `tel:` link to call; no late fee runs on its own), the rental days (`rentalDays`; each becomes final at the day close, Phase 3, AT-14) and "What happened" (`rentalHistory`, the `rental_event` rows newest first; each kind needs a sentence under `rentals.history.kinds`). The bill, payments section, receipt, correction, earnings split, Extend and Receive come with their tickets; imported and started-before-go-live rentals (AT-37, AT-42) with the starting-data import.
- Approvals (`src/modules/approvals/`, #56; 05.7 on the rental page, 10.1 at `/approvals`, 10.2 to 10.4 at `/approvals/[id]?do=approve|reject`): `askForPrice` (Customer access, rental draft or active) saves `approval_request` (insert-only: a new daily rate LKR 1 to 100,000 or a discount LKR 1 up to the estimate, the reason, the rate and estimate when asked); one waits per rental (`waitingFor`), its sender may `withdraw` it. `decide` needs `changePriceOrDiscount`, never on one's own request except the primary owner (`self_approved`); rejecting needs a note; a rate above a partner car's package (`aboveTheirPackage`) also needs `sharePartnerCarSurcharge` and the partner's rupees of the extra (0 to the whole extra) with a reason. The decision is `approval_decision` (insert-only, one per request; approved, rejected, withdrawn, or closed when the rental ends first: `closeWaiting`, called by `freeCar` and later by cancel, #59). The reason is kept word for word (only trimmed). The commission and the partner's share show only with `seePartnerEarnings`. The saved terms never change: `approvedChanges` (rentals.ts) gives the newest approved rate and the approved discounts, which `rentalEstimate` (so payments, the printout and pages) uses; `approvedTerms` adds the extra and its split for the day close (Phase 3). Approving while draft moves `rental.terms_set_at` (when step 4 is set), so the signed agreement is out of date. Logged `approval.*` (hidden, with `rental.*`, from people without Customer access) and `rental_event` `priceRequest`/`approval`/`approvalWithdrawn`. The menu's Approvals count (`waitingCount`) is for people who may decide. Later approval kinds (corrections #65, swaps #62, damage and early return #63) add their own `kind`.
- Return and the bill (`rentals/returns.ts`, #63; 07.1 and 07.2 at `/rentals/[id]/return`, the bill on the rental page): `receiveCar` (Customer access; car then rental locks; rental `active`) checks the incoming km (not below `out_km`), fuel, the handover's own accessory list (item by item, `checked`), the late charge (only when late: an amount, or waived with a reason), same-level fuel (whole litres × the saved price, or waived), each accessory missing since handover (an amount, or 0 with a reason), damage, garage days (only with damage and the car going Under repair) and the next state; it saves `rental_return` and the bill as `charge` lines (both insert-only) with `allocation`: `commission` (partner share by `splitShare`), `split` (the admin's rupees, set with an approval), `sns`, or `admin` (damage and accessories on a partner car: a `split` request waits on Approvals). Rent, long-rental extra, the approved discount, extra km and late follow the commission; the extra above a partner car's package follows the #56 split; fuel is all SNS's. Unpaid after payments and the deposit held needs `allowPaymentAfterReturn` and a note. It sets the segment's `in_km`, the rental `returned`, ends the hold, writes the next car status, closes a waiting price request, and logs `rental.return`. Garage days are an approval (`garageDays`, `allocateDamageProceeds`; on a partner car with the partner's rupees); `correctGarageDays` adds a dated line for the difference. `rentalBill` reads the lines with their shares; payments on a returned rental are capped by the bill (`billTotal`). Partner earning lines are made from these lines with the day close (Phase 3); using the deposit and refunding is #64 (below); early-return reductions #76.
- Debt warning (#77, AT-55): `debtOf` (customers/flags.ts) is each returned rental's bill less payments less the deposit held, when above 0; `warningsFor` includes it, so New rental (05.1), the check before a rental (08.4) and the customer page show it. Continuing needs `proceedDespiteFlag` and a reason; `customer_go_ahead.debt` keeps the amount, and `createRental` accepts the decision only for exactly that debt (a payment in between needs a new decision). Write-offs (#65) are subtracted there (figures.ts).
- Deposit settlement (`money/deposits.ts`, #64, AT-12, AT-56; 07.3 at `/rentals/[id]/deposit`, the result under the bill): `settleDeposit` (Customer access; customer then rental locks; rental `returned`; refused while garage days wait, `garageWaiting`) saves one `deposit_settlement` per rental and `deposit_movement` rows: `applied` to this bill first, then, with `olderAgreed` and a note, to the customer's older unpaid bills oldest first (`to_rental_id`; the plan is `planDeposit`, `src/lib/deposit-plan.ts`, shared with the form), and the rest `refunded` (a refund above 0 needs `refundsAndExpenses`; cash adds a `cash_movement` `refundOut`, bank needs its reference). The form sends the amounts it showed; anything changed is `stale`. Logged `rental.depositSettle`, a `rental_event` `depositSettled`, and `depositFromOther` on each older rental paid. A deposit used for a bill counts as paid on it: since #65 every reader adds money up in one place, `moneyOf` (`money/figures.ts`).
- Money figures (`money/figures.ts`, #65): `moneyOf`/`moneyOne` give each rental's paid (payments + approved `payment_adjustment` lines + deposit used for it − money paid back), held (deposit received and `adjusted` − used and refunded) and written off. `paidAndHeld`, the payment cap (`paidOn`: paid + written off), `debtOf` and `depositState` all read it: a new kind of money line goes there, nowhere else. Its subqueries name `"rental"."id"` in full (a bare column would be read from the inner table).
- Payment corrections (`money/corrections.ts`, #65; 09.5 at `/rentals/[id]/payments/[paymentId]/correct`, decided on Approvals): `askCorrection` (Customer access; customer then rental locks) saves an `approval_request` of kind `correction` (`payment_id`, `fix`: amount, method, reference or rental; `estimate_was` is the part's amount; one waits per payment part); `decideCorrection` (`correctPayment`; `decide` hands corrections to it) checks the fix again and writes a `payment_adjustment` (bill) or `deposit_movement` `adjusted` (deposit, only before settlement), a `cash_movement` `correctionIn`/`correctionOut` for cash differences, and a `reconciliation` `waiting` for a new bank or card way or reference. The payment row never changes; `partOf` gives the part as corrected (newest approved way and reference). Logged `payment.correctionAsk`, `approval.approve`/`reject`; rental events `correctionRequest`, `correctionMovedIn`, `approval` (shown as `correctionDecided`).
- Write-offs and paying back (`money/writeoffs.ts`, #65, AT-17; 08.6 at `/customers/[id]/write-off`, pay back at `/rentals/[id]/pay-back`): `writeOffDebt` (`writeOffDebt` permission, the owner until given; the debt shown must hold) saves `write_off` and `write_off_line` (oldest rentals first); the bill stays and the written-off part can no longer be paid. `payBack` (`refundsAndExpenses` and Customer access) pays back up to `paidAbove` (paid − bill on a returned rental) as a `refund` (`overpaid`; cash adds a `cash_movement` `refundOut` with `refund_id`); the form's key (`refund.idempotency_key`) pays back once when sent twice. A payment moved by a correction is listed on the rental it moved to (`rentalMoney`). All insert-only. AT-16 and AT-84 come with the day close (Phase 3), AT-38 with the starting-data import.
