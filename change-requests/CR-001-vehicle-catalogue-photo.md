## What

Each vehicle may have **one optional photo** of the actual car. An admin adds or replaces it on the edit-car screen. The photo is shown on the vehicle's card and profile. Until a photo is added, the system shows the model's standard image (Wagon R, Alto, Spacia, Every, Prius, Axio, or a general car).

It is **not**:

- a vehicle-condition photo
- a handover requirement
- a customer document

## Why

Staff and the owner find a car faster when its card shows the real car, for example its colour. The standard model images already cover every car, so the photo is optional and nothing is blocked without it.

- Handover requirements (§6)
- Optional condition photos at handover (§6)
- Prices, phases, payments and the agreement
