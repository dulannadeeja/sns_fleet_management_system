# ADD-003 · Optional catalogue photo per car

Addendum to the SNS Rent a Car proposal (v1, approved). Change request: `change-requests/CR-001-vehicle-catalogue-photo.md`.

**Agreed by:** S D De Silva, owner. Recorded by Dulan Nadeeja, 26 October 2026.

By agreement the system also includes:

- One optional photo of the actual car, added or replaced by an admin on the edit-car screen, shown on the car's card and page.
- Until a photo is added, the model's standard image is shown.
- It is not a condition photo, not a handover requirement and not a customer document.

**Price:** no change. **Phase:** Phase 1 (Foundation). **Timeline:** no change.

Everything else in the proposal stands as approved.
