# ADD-002 · Starting data

Addendum to the SNS Rent a Car proposal (v1, approved). Change request: `change-requests/CR-003-starting-data.md`.

**Agreed by:** S D De Silva, owner. Recorded by Dulan Nadeeja.

The proposal's list of what is not included named entering figures from the old books, and said the system starts from zero. By agreement, the system now starts with SNS's existing data:

- Customers, cars and packages, and partner owners.
- Opening balances on go-live day: drawer cash, customer debts, partner balances owed, staff advances and loans. None counts as income.
- Past rentals, payments, expenses and payroll, marked "Imported" and shown in history and reports.

The developer loads the data once before go-live; the owner approves it.

**Price:** no change. **Phase:** Phase 4 (Live). **Timeline:** no change.

Everything else in the proposal stands as approved.
