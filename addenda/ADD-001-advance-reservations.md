# ADD-001 · Advance reservations

Addendum to the SNS Rent a Car proposal (v1, approved). Change request: `change-requests/CR-002-advance-reservations.md`.

**Agreed by:** S D De Silva, owner. Recorded by Dulan Nadeeja.

The proposal's list of what is not included named advance booking. By agreement it is now included:

- Staff can reserve a specific car for a customer up to 30 days ahead, by phone or at the counter.
- An advance payment is optional. Cancellation and no-show follow the existing cancellation rule.
- At pickup the reservation becomes a normal rental, with every check, the signed agreement and the handover requirements.

**Price:** no change. **Phase:** Phase 2 (Rentals). **Timeline:** no change.

Everything else in the proposal stands as approved.
