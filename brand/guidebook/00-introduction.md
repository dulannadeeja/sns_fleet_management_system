# 00 · Introduction

Plate is the visual system for the SNS Rent Car management website, chosen by the owner on 24 Oct 2026. It sets the look only: screens, flows, words, permissions and money rules come from the system design and the screens.

It is used by the owner, one admin and three staff at one branch near Elpitiya, on office computers and phones, in Sinhala first. The idea is in the name: **the Sri Lankan number plate is the one thing that stands out.** Every car is shown by its plate, on a yellow plate face; everything else stays quiet.

**Who it is for and where it runs:** the owner, one admin and three staff, on office computers and phones, Sinhala first. Requirements and business rules live in `SNS_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md`; this guidebook covers how SNS looks, reads and sounds.
