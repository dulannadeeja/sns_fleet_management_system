A real map that draws any trip

Real OpenStreetMap roads, towns and coastline, in our colours. Zoom in, drag, tap a stop. The route follows real roads and every leg shows road kilometres and drive time. Nothing is drawn by hand: each trip is just a list of places.

Drive times are routing estimates for a car. Real Sri Lankan drives are often slower (bends, towns, tuk-tuks), so the real site should add a safety margin. Place positions are approximate for this mockup.

How it works

Map data, hosted by us

Sri Lanka's OpenStreetMap data (zoom 0–11) is one 7.4 MB file we host ourselves. No Google or Mapbox account, no fee per map view. Shown with MapLibre, a free open-source map library.

Places once, routes as lists

Each place has a name and position. A trip is a list of place ids and nights. The admin trip builder makes the list; the map draws it.

Road legs saved once

Road km, drive time and the road line for each pair of places come from a routing service (OSRM, open source) once, and are saved. Pages never wait for it.
