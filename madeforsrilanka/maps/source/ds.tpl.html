Maps

One map part, RouteMap, draws every trip on a real map from a list of places. It is never drawn by hand. The live website will use Google Maps; mockups use OpenStreetMap because claude.ai blocks Google map images. Everything on this page applies to both.

Two states

Plan mode on trip pages and the quiz result. Trip mode on the guest trip page. Drag, zoom (Ctrl or two fingers), or tap a stop.

Plan: The ClassicWhole route lime, every stop white. Used where guests choose a trip.

Trip: day 4 of The ClassicDriven legs and visited stops lime; the rest white. Used on the guest trip page.

Map colours

The base map is quiet so the lime route stands out. These colours are only for the map; they are also on the colour palette page.

Behaviour

First viewFits the whole route with 48 px space around it. Never shows more than Sri Lanka.
Scrolling the pageThe page scrolls; the map needs Ctrl + scroll or two fingers, so guests never get trapped in it.
Tap a stop or a dayFlies to that stop (0.9 s). Tap again to see the whole route.
Change the tripRoute and stops redraw, the map refits (0.8 s).
Reduce motionJumps instead of flying.
Map can't loadA plain drawn island with the same numbered route appears instead; the day list always works.
PhonesDay list comes first; the map supports it. "Open in Google Maps" for directions.

Data

A place is saved once. A trip is a list. The map only reads.

place:  id, name (EN/DE), lat, lng, google_place_id
route:  stops: [ {place, nights}, … ]
leg:    from, to, road_km, drive_min, line
RouteMap(stops, { mode: "plan" | "trip", visited })
Day cards"154 km · about 2 h 58 by road" from the saved leg
Drive timesRouting estimates plus a safety margin (amount still open)
Trip cards, PDFs, share cardsA still image of the same map
Driver directions"Open in Google Maps" link with every stop

Moving to Google Maps

  1. Same parts, same colours: route, stops and names stay as on this page; Google's map is styled with cloud map styling to match the colours above.
  2. Road legs from Google's Routes API. Check Google's terms first: Google routes must be shown on a Google map, and saving Google results long term is limited.
  3. Still images for cards and PDFs from Google's Static Maps.
  4. Cost: free allowance of about 10,000 map loads a month for the standard map, then paid; check current prices before launch.
  5. Keep Google's logo and terms link visible on every map.

Rules

  1. Never draw a map for one route. Fix a place in the library and every map updates.
  2. The route is the only bright thing on the map: lime with an ink edge. No other coloured markers.
  3. Stops are numbered in visiting order and match the day list.
  4. Kotiya never sits on a map, and prices never appear on it.
  5. Always show the map credit.
  6. Drive times always say "about" and include the safety margin.
