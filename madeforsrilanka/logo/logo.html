A logo grown from Kotiya

The logo is not the mascot. It sits on every page, including price, payment and Help, where Kotiya must stay away. So each mark keeps one piece of Kotiya and drops the cartoon. That piece must still read at 16 pixels in a browser tab.

Recommended: Kotiya Tile

Our lime square becomes Kotiya's face: two leopard ears with dark backs, eyes, a nose, and one rosette on the forehead so it reads as a leopard, not a cat. It keeps the lime you already own and stays calm on every page.

How it is built

  1. Grid of 64 units. Tile 52 × 48, corner radius 16, the same curve as our cards.
  2. Ears are circles of radius 9, half hidden behind the tile.
  3. Eyes, nose and the forehead rosette sit on the centre line, so the face never looks crooked. Two cheek rosettes make it a leopard, not a bear or cat.
  4. Below 48 px (favicon, website header) the rosettes are left out: only ears, eyes and nose. Small sizes stay clean.
  5. Clear space around the logo: at least the height of one ear on every side.
