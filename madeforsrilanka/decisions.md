- Registration: not registered yet -> start as Pvt Ltd company. Order: company -> SLTDA -> gateway/marketplaces
- PRICING MODEL (practical, from owner):

  - Driver: two levels: driver only LKR 4,000/day; driver-guide (English/German) LKR 6,000/day; meals + room NOT included -> we pay, built into price
