Travel guides

Guides bring people from Google, answer their questions honestly, and lead them to a trip. They are written by us, from the road, in our voice. Every guide ends at a real trip or day trip with a live price.

Two types of guide

Place page

For every place: Ella, Galle, Yala, Elpitiya and the rest.

  1. Drone clip or wide photoOur own, never stock
  2. Why go, in 3 linesMoment first: what you'll feel there
  3. Best time to goA 12-month bar: dry, mixed, rainy
  4. Top 5 things to doEach with an honest insider tip, including what to skip and the traps
  5. Drive timesFrom the airport and nearby towns, from the places list
  6. Our trips and day trips thereLive "from" prices
  7. MapThe real map with this place and nearby stops
  8. Three questionsShort answers people search for

Planning guide

For big questions: "How much does a private driver cost in Sri Lanka in 2027?", "Sri Lanka in 7 days", "Best time for the south coast".

  1. The answer firstOne short paragraph with the number, the date or the route
  2. A table or the live calculatorPrices from the system, never typed
  3. Explain the partsShort sections with clear headings
  4. Honest warningsTraps, seasons, what we would not do
  5. One sample tripMap, days and live price; "Try it: swap a day"
  6. Questions and sourcesOfficial sites linked for visas, parks and fees
  7. Plan my tripOne clear button at the end, quiz embedded on long guides

    Questions

    How many nights in Ella?

    Two. One night means you arrive tired and leave before the morning mist clears.

    Do I need to book the train?

    Reserved seats sell out in busy months. We book them for you as part of the trip.

    Is Ella too touristy?

    The town centre is busy. Walk 20 minutes in any direction and it's tea pickers and birdsong.

    Writing

    1. Written by us, from our own visits. Say when we were last there.
    2. Answer first, then explain. No long intros about "the pearl of the Indian Ocean".
    3. Every top-5 item has a real tip, including what to skip: queues, traps, overpriced spots.
    4. Prices, km and times come from the system, not typed. Example numbers say "sample".
    5. Facts that change (visa rules, park fees, opening hours) link to the official source and carry a "checked" date.
    6. Follow the voice and tone page: short sentences, everyday words, energy without hype.
    7. No "best", "No. 1" or "guaranteed sightings". No naming or attacking other companies.
    8. German version written by a person, with the same facts.
