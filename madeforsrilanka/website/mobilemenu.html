Three phone menus

Today the phone shows only the logo and "Plan my trip", with no way to reach Trips, Day trips, Guides or the crew. Here are three menus in our design. Tap the menu button in each phone to open it, tap again to close.

What real sites do

Perk (our base)

Two-line menu button. Opens a white rounded card under the bar with rows and arrows, a lime button and an outline button. The page behind blurs. This is option A.

Tripadvisor

Rounded white sheet from the bottom, one dark main button, outline buttons below. The pattern for option C.

Kiwi.com

Menu panel with "My trips" at the top and simple text rows. Like our "My trip" row.

Zara

Full-screen menu with very large words. Bold and brand-led, like option B.

My recommendation: A. It continues the Perk menu you chose (flat at the top, floating bar when scrolling), so the menu card grows straight out of that bar. "Plan my trip" stays as the lime button, and WhatsApp sits right under it. C is a good second choice if most visitors use one hand on the beach.
