Motion and animation

Less motion

Some people feel sick or distracted by movement and turn on "Reduce motion" on their phone or computer. The website respects it.

MotionWith "Reduce motion" on
Hero videoDoes not start by itself; shows the first frame and a Play button
Place marqueeStops; the names can be scrolled by hand
Kotiya's bounce, confettiOff; Kotiya just changes face
Sheets, toasts, chips, progressChange at once, no sliding
Smooth scrolling to a sectionJumps straight there

Rules

  1. Motion shows that something happened. It never decorates and never blocks.
  2. Nothing waits for an animation: buttons work at once, and text is readable before any motion ends.
  3. Use the four speeds and two curves above. No bounce or spring outside Kotiya.
  4. Only Kotiya plays: a small bounce when its face changes, confetti once after booking. Never near prices, payment or Help.
  5. Move only position, size and fade (transform and opacity), so phones stay smooth.
  6. Anything that moves for more than 5 seconds can be paused (video) or stops on hover (marquee).
  7. Always respect "Reduce motion".
  8. No pop-ups that slide in by themselves, no auto-scrolling carousels, no flashing.
Source: the website mockup and DESIGN.md (Fresh), 23 November 2026. The first DESIGN brief said 150–250 ms with a card lift, Kotiya reacting, the route drawing itself and small confetti on booking; this page makes that exact.
