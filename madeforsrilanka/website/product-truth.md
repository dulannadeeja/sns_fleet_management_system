# MadeForSriLanka — product truth

Source: the owner's decisions recorded in the "MadeForSriLanka Source of Truth" doc (2026-11-22).

## What it is
Private Sri Lanka trips run by three young local partners based on the south coast (Galle/Elpitiya). Round tours (Quick Taste 5n, The Classic 7n, Deep Dive 10n, The Full Island 14n), day tours from the south coast, driver per day, airport transfers, safe "night out" rides, village homestays, a Raptor 4x4 adventure.

## Audience
First: European couples and families, 30–55, first visit, planning 3–6 months ahead on a laptop, comparing 3–5 companies. Second: tourists already on the south coast, on a phone, booking for tomorrow.

## What they must believe
Honest price with no commission traps; they can change the plan anytime, even mid-trip; the same driver looks after them 24/7. Real Sri Lanka shown by locals, with energy.

## Brand commitments (keep)
- Name and wordmark "MadeForSriLanka" ("MadeFor" + "SriLanka"), line-drawn elephant logo mark from the current site.
- Tagline: "Your Sri Lanka. Your way. Honest price."
- Mascot: an original friendly Sri Lankan leopard (quiz guide).
- Real Sri Lanka imagery only (owner's drone and travel videos); no stock, no AI images, no other countries.
- Plain easy English (non-native readers), German "du". No unproven claims ("best", awards, licence until issued).
- Experience-first: moment headlines before place names; 6 vibes (Wild, Calm, Romantic, Social, Adventurous, Real local); light pages, details on tap; guided path Vibe → Trip & price → Book.

## Must not feel like
An old tour-company site (walls of text, "Day 1: visit X"), a generic SaaS landing page, or AI-template travel (cream + serif + terracotta, gradient blobs).

## Surfaces
Website (Persuade): home, vibe explorer, package page with live price panel, quiz, quick booking. Guest trip page (Operate). Driver app and admin (Operate).
