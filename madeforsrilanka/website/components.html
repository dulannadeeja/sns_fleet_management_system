Layout

One container, one side margin, three screen sizes.

Laptop, from 1000 px: 4 cards a row (trips, promises). Side panels become sticky.

Tablet, 760–999 px: 2 cards a row.

Phone, under 760 px: 1 card a row, sticky price bar at the bottom.

Content width1280 px at most, centred
Side margin16 px, more if the phone has a notch or side bar
Reading width56–64 characters
Breakpointsphone under 760 · tablet 760 · laptop 1000 · small phone under 430 (smaller logo and buttons)
SectionsStone sections and full-width white bands take turns (see Section backgrounds in DESIGN.md)

Corners

Round corners are part of the look. Bigger things get bigger corners; anything you tap to choose is fully round.

Shadows

Only things that float above the page get a shadow. Cards sit flat and are set apart by colour.
