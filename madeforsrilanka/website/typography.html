Rules

  1. Sentence case everywhere: "Plan my trip", not "Plan My Trip". No all-caps text.
  2. No small labels above headings. The heading says it.
  3. Headlines: Medium 500, letter spacing −0.03 em, line height 0.95 to 1.1. Body: Regular 400, line height 1.55. Sizes follow Perk: 80 px page titles and 60 px section titles at most.
  4. Reading lines stay short: 56 to 64 characters wide at most.
  5. Nothing smaller than 12 px, and nothing Sinhala smaller than 14 px.
  6. Prices and other numbers always use tabular figures.
  7. One big page title (H1) per page.
  8. German text runs about 30% longer than English. Buttons and cards must wrap without breaking the layout.
  9. Text colours follow the palette page: ink, grey text, or lime on ink. Never lime or ochre text on light grounds.
  10. The logo wordmark is drawn shapes. Never retype "MadeForSriLanka" as the logo.
