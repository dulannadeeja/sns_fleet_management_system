Rules

  1. Text is ink on stone, white, lime, cream or ochre, or lime on ink.
  2. Lime and ochre are never text on white or stone. They are fills only.
  3. Ochre, cream and the prop colours belong to Kotiya and the logo ears. They never become buttons, backgrounds or text.
  4. Kotiya always sits on a stone or white tile, never directly on lime (the bandana would vanish) or on a photo with a real leopard.
  5. Logo ear centres are always ink, also on the ink tile.
  6. The website stays light. Only the design pages have a dark theme.
  7. No new colour without adding it to this page and to DESIGN.md first.
Source: DESIGN.md (Fresh), 23 November 2026. Contrast follows WCAG 2.1: 4.5 to 1 for normal text, 3 to 1 for large text and shapes.
