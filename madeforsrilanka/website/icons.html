Icons

One simple line style for every icon: drawn on a 24 by 24 grid, rounded ends, no fills. They take the colour of the text around them. Tap "Copy SVG" to copy an icon's code.

Style

  • 24 × 24 grid, 2 px safe edge
  • Line 2.2 px at 24 px (2.4–2.6 px for small arrows and ticks)
  • Round ends and round corners
  • Outline only. Filled only for play and pause.
  • Colour: currentColor

The line weight is close to the strokes of Onest Medium text, so icons and words look like one family. Round ends match our pill buttons and round cards. No emoji, no clip-art, no brand logos inside the website.

The logo and Kotiya are drawings, not icons. They have their own pages.

In use

How icons sit inside real parts of the website.

Buttons: arrow after the words, 16 px. The Help button never gets an icon of its own.

Facts on cards: 15 px grey icon before 14 px grey text.

Promise cards: 22–24 px ink icon on a 44 px lime tile.

Icon-only buttons always carry a spoken name for screen readers ("Open trip", "Back", "Pause video").

Rules

  1. Only icons from this page. A new icon is drawn in the same style and added here first.
  2. An icon sits next to words. Icon-only buttons (arrow, back, pause, close) must have a spoken name.
  3. Icons take the text colour: ink, grey, or white on dark. Never lime or ochre on light grounds.
  4. One icon per meaning: "pin" is always a place, "clock" always a duration, "undo" always a change you can take back.
  5. No emoji and no text symbols (✓, ✕, ★) as icons. Draw them.
  6. WhatsApp uses our chat bubble, not the WhatsApp logo, so the button matches the site. If the official logo is used later, follow WhatsApp's brand rules.
  7. No star ratings drawn by us. Reviews show the source's own score (Google, TripAdvisor) as text.
