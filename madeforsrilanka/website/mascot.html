Where it helps, where it stays away

A guide earns trust by knowing when to be quiet. Rule: at most one leopard on a screen, and never next to money or safety.

Shows up

  • Quiz questions, reactions and the result
  • "Welcome back, your trip is saved"
  • Small hints: "Not sure? Tap a vibe"
  • Empty and lost pages: "This page wandered off into Yala"
  • Leaflet QR pages, Instagram share cards, stickers on guest videos

Stays away

  • The hero: the real video does the talking
  • Price breakdown, payment and deposit screens
  • The Help button, breakdowns, sickness, lost passport
  • Driver app and admin dashboard
  • Next to real wildlife photos (a cartoon beside a real leopard looks cheap)

What the illustrator gets

Brief

  1. Original Sri Lankan leopard, front-facing, sitting. Must not look like any existing mascot.
  2. Shape style: flat colours, rounded shapes, open rosettes, no gradients.
  3. Colours: leopard ochre, cream, ink lines and spots, lime bandana.
  4. Must read clearly at 48 px, the size of the quiz bubble.
  5. Company owns all rights, written in the contract.

Files

  1. Main pose, plus a head-and-paws "peek" version.
  2. 6 faces: happy, curious, excited, thinking, sleepy, oops.
  3. 6 personalities with their props.
  4. SVG for the website, PNG for Instagram, a small sticker set.
  5. Simple motion: blink, tail flick, head tilt (Lottie or CSS).
Sketches by Claude for direction only. Mobbin links are references, not designs to copy.
